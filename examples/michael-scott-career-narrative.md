# Michael Scott — Career Narrative

I am a people-first sales leader who believes that business is personal. My
career at Dunder Mifflin began in sales, where I learned that the best account
relationships are built by listening, remembering what matters to the customer,
and showing up when a competitor would rather send an email. That approach made
me a top-performing salesperson and eventually led to my role as Regional
Manager of the Scranton branch.

As Regional Manager, I led a team across sales, accounting, customer service,
quality assurance, warehouse operations, and administration. My style was
unconventional, but my goal was consistent: create a workplace where people
felt known, keep the team together through change, and deliver results. The
Scranton branch remained resilient through downsizing, leadership transitions,
a corporate merger, and shifting expectations from headquarters. We retained
important accounts, integrated new colleagues, and became one of the company's
strongest-performing branches.

I also founded the Michael Scott Paper Company. Building a competitor from the
ground up taught me how to identify an underserved customer need, move quickly,
win business through relationships, and negotiate from a position that was much
stronger than it looked on paper. The company was ultimately acquired by Dunder
Mifflin, and our team returned with jobs and restored client relationships.

The thread through my career is loyalty: to customers, to colleagues, and to
the belief that work can be both productive and deeply human. I do my best work
in roles where I can coach a team, build lasting relationships, rally people
through uncertainty, and make sure no important meeting is ever short on energy.

_Fictional sample based on Michael Scott from NBC's The Office._
