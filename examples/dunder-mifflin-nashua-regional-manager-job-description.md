# Regional Manager — Nashua Branch

**Dunder Mifflin Paper Company**

Nashua, New Hampshire · Full-time · On-site

## About Dunder Mifflin

Dunder Mifflin provides paper, office supplies, and personalized service to
businesses throughout the Northeast. We compete by knowing our customers,
responding quickly, and delivering the level of attention that larger suppliers
cannot match.

## The Opportunity

We are seeking a Regional Manager to lead our Nashua branch. This person will
own branch performance, coach a customer-focused sales team, and ensure that
sales, accounting, customer service, administration, and warehouse operations
work together effectively.

The ideal candidate combines strong commercial judgment with an approachable,
hands-on leadership style. They can protect long-standing accounts, find new
business in a competitive market, and guide a close-knit team through changing
priorities without losing momentum.

## Responsibilities

- Own branch revenue, account retention, operating performance, and day-to-day
  execution.
- Lead, coach, and motivate sales representatives to meet individual and branch
  targets.
- Maintain relationships with key customers and step into sensitive account
  conversations when needed.
- Develop local sales strategies for paper, office supplies, and related
  business services.
- Review pipeline activity, forecasts, pricing decisions, and customer risks
  with the sales team.
- Coordinate across sales, accounting, customer service, administration, and
  warehouse operations.
- Recruit, onboard, develop, and retain branch employees.
- Communicate corporate priorities clearly while representing the needs of the
  Nashua team to senior leadership.
- Manage organizational change with empathy, consistency, and sound judgment.
- Build a professional, inclusive workplace where employees are recognized and
  held accountable.
- Travel locally for customer meetings and occasionally to corporate or other
  regional branches.

## Qualifications

- Five or more years of experience in business-to-business sales, account
  management, or branch operations.
- Two or more years of experience leading a sales team or multidisciplinary
  business unit.
- Demonstrated success retaining customers and growing revenue in a competitive
  market.
- Strong coaching, negotiation, conflict-resolution, and presentation skills.
- Ability to interpret sales performance, forecasts, margins, and operating
  metrics.
- Comfortable making decisions independently while working within company
  policies.
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills.
- Bachelor's degree or equivalent combination of experience and professional
  achievement.

## Preferred Qualifications

- Experience in paper, office supplies, distribution, or a related industry.
- Existing relationships with businesses in southern New Hampshire or northern
  Massachusetts.
- Experience leading through a merger, reorganization, or other significant
  operational change.
- A record of developing salespeople into high performers and future leaders.

## What Success Looks Like

In the first year, the Regional Manager will strengthen the branch's operating
rhythm, improve visibility into the sales pipeline, retain priority accounts,
and establish a clear plan for responsible growth. Just as importantly, the
Nashua team will understand what is expected, feel supported in doing its best
work, and know that leadership is present when it matters.

## Compensation and Benefits

- Competitive base salary with performance-based bonus
- Health, dental, and vision coverage
- Paid time off and company holidays
- Retirement plan with company contribution
- Mileage reimbursement for customer travel
- Employee pricing on select paper and office-supply products
